## Brand & Style

The brand personality is inviting, cheerful, and delicately refined—capturing the tactile pleasure of handcrafted pastries, spun sugar, and fresh strawberry glaze. Designed for confectionery enthusiasts, bespoke celebration planners, and casual dessert lovers, the UI evokes feelings of indulgence, warmth, and effortless sophistication.

The visual style merges Tactile Softness with Modern Confectionery Minimalism:
- Generous cream-toned negative space that breathes like a clean pastry marble counter.
- Rounded, organic contours echoing smooth fondant, extruded piping, and pill-shaped packaging.
- Subtle luminous warmth that makes imagery pop with appetizing bakery luster while maintaining pristine legibility.

## Colors

The color palette translates classic patisserie ingredients into a cohesive interface:

- **Primary (`#E86F68`)**: Fresh strawberry blush. Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicator states, highlighted price tags, and focal badges.
- **Secondary (`#EBB382`)**: Warm butter and baked pastry crust. Ideal for accent ribbons, promotional banners, celebratory tags, and warm interactive hover fills.
- **Tertiary (`#768E70`)**: Grounding pistachio green. Deployed selectively for herbal notes, freshness callouts, dietary tags (e.g., vegan, gluten-free), and delicate contrast.
- **Neutral (`#54433E`)**: Rich roasted espresso bean. Serves as the high-contrast foundational tone for text, icons, and precise borders, replacing cold artificial blacks with organic warmth.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base**: Soft vanilla cream (`#FDFBF7`) providing an ambient, luminous glow.
- **Card/Container Surface**: Whipped cream white (`#FFFFFF`) with soft blushing tinted borders.
- **Subtle Surface Muted**: Glaze wash (`#F9F1EC`) for pill chips, table rows, and secondary backgrounds.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies on the rounded geometry and open counters of **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a crisp, warm, and distinctly approachable voice. 

- Large display headlines utilize bold weights (`700` and `800`) with tight tracking (`-0.02em`) to mirror the plumpness of crafted cakes and confections.
- Body text remains grounded at regular weight (`400`) set in rich roasted espresso (`#54433E`) to guarantee AAA contrast against vanilla backgrounds.
- Micro-labels, bakery categorization tags, and allergen pills leverage medium-to-bold weights with uppercase or expanded tracking (`0.02em`–`0.04em`) for rapid scanning during custom cake configuration.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a flexible, modular fluid grid grounded on an 8pt base grid rhythm:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer canvas margins.

Generous vertical padding between sections (`space-xl` multiplied by 1.5–2) creates a calm, boutique shopping experience. Elements inside cards cluster tightly via `space-xs` and `space-sm`, emphasizing groupings of dietary tags, prices, and cake specifications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy adopts soft ambient warmth, avoiding sterile grey dropshadows in favor of strawberry- and caramel-tinted diffusions:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Pure flush surfaces on vanilla backgrounds, defined by a 1px soft border in `#EEDFD6`.
- **Level 1 (Pastry Cards & Inputs)**: Tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(84, 67, 62, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(232, 111, 104, 0.04)`. Imparts a gentle levitation from the counter.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Actions & Dropdowns)**: Deeper glow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(84, 67, 62, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(232, 111, 104, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Cake Customizer & Cart Drawer)**: Dramatic warm presence: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(84, 67, 62, 0.16)`. Combined with a backdrop blur of `8px` overlaid with `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped structural archetype (`roundedness: 3`):

- **Buttons, Badges, and Form Chips**: Full pill contours (`border-radius: 9999px`) creating approachable, candy-coated touch targets.
- **Standard Cards & Floating Containers**: Styled with `rounded-lg` (2rem / 32px) to communicate soft-baked warmth.
- **Hero Banners & Feature Highlights**: Styled with `rounded-xl` (3rem / 48px) for prominent, bakery-window framing.
- **Imagery**: Cake photography uses soft asymmetric arches or gentle rounded-lg pill cutouts to highlight textures of frostings, piping, and fresh fruit garnishes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill radius, strawberry blush background (`#E86F68`), crisp white text, and a soft coral hover shadow. On active click, scales subtly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary**: Full pill radius, translucent butter glaze background (`#FDF1E6`), neutral espresso text (`#54433E`), and a 1px border (`#EBB382`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Espresso text on transparent background with a rounded highlight container appearing on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Confectionery pills with `border-radius: 9999px` and compact padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-md` horizontal).
- Flavor chips (e.g., "Salted Caramel", "Pistachio Rose") use subtle muted fills with corresponding border tints. Selected chips transition to solid strawberry blush with white typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Pill-shaped inputs (`h-12` or `h-14`) with a warm off-white fill (`#FFFFFF`), neutral border (`#EEDFD6`), and espresso text.
- Focus rings display a soft 3px strawberry blush glow (`rgba(232, 111, 104, 0.25)`) with an inner crisp stroke (`#E86F68`).

### Cards (Product & Cake Display)
- Pure white container with `rounded-lg` (2rem) borders and soft Level 1 tinted shadows.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg`), image containers with subtle inset pill curves, floating quick-order pill buttons, and explicit allergen/flavor tags positioned atop pastry photography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radios and checkboxes maintain pill-like softness (`rounded-full` for radios, `rounded-md` for checks).
- In checked state, surfaces fill with strawberry blush (`#E86F68`) containing a crisp white tick or dot.

### Cake Customizer Additions
- **Flavor & Tier Steppers**: Circular pill counters (`+` / `-`) encased in warm butter tone accents.
- **Date & Pick-up Slot Selector**: Horizontal scrollable pill containers showing delivery dates, highlighting selected bakery slots in warm strawberry tones.
